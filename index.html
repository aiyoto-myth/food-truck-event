Where the checks come from

  • Break-even under 60% of a normal event is comfortable; over 80%, say no. From KitchenNmbrs' guide "How do I know if an event or festival is worth it financially".
  • Sales should be at least 4× what you pay the organizer. The UK caterers' association NCASS calls it the "Times Four" rule.
  • Commission 10% or less; never over 15%; over 10% should come with a sales guarantee; a flat fee under US$300 a day; a flat fee or a commission, never both. From the Washington State Food Truck Association.
  • A slow event shouldn't cost you money, and it should pay you for your own hours.

Rules of thumb from the trade, not financial advice. You know your truck; the receipt just makes the guess honest.
